## About Mathrix
### EPSILON TO INFINITY
        Mathrix is the annual intercollegiate symposium where mathematics meets technology! Hosted by the Department of Mathematics, College of Engineering Guindy, Anna University, Mathrix is a one-of-a-kind fest that brings together logic, innovation, and problem-solving under one roof.

        Organized by the brilliant minds of M.Sc. Computer Science (5 years), M.Sc. Information Technology (5 years), and M.Sc. Mathematics (2 years), Mathrix is more than just a symposium—it's a fusion of numbers, algorithms, and cutting-edge tech.

        With a perfect blend of mathematical and technical events, this fest is designed to challenge intellects, spark creativity, and push boundaries. From thrilling competitions that test your mathematical prowess to tech-driven challenges that fuel your coding skills, Mathrix is the ultimate arena for innovators, problem-solvers, and future disruptors.
